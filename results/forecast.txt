8. ASSUMPTIONS & LIMITS
   - Statewide center = poll average; poll error SD 4.5 pts on margin, fat-tailed (Texas misses 2018-24: 3-4.5 pts).
   - County pattern = 50/50 blend of 2018 and 2024 Senate results relative to the state; regions drift together.
   - Region noise 2-2.2 pts (4 pts for Hispanic South/West, which swung ~9.5 pts 2018->2024); county noise 1.8 pts.
   - Turnout = 2018 county turnout rates x Oct 2026 registration (19.04M), +/-7% statewide, +/-5% by region.
   - 2026 primary turnout nudges regions (15% weight). SD-9 special (D+31 swing) not used directly.
   - Early vote turnout: in-person voting starts Oct 19; not yet in the model.
   - Third parties ignored (two-party shares).
