The '''2026 Texas gubernatorial election''' is scheduled to take place on November 3, 2026, to elect the [[governor of Texas]]. [[Republican Party (United States)|Republican]] incumbent [[Greg Abbott]] is seeking a fourth term.<ref name=absolutely>{{cite web |url= https://www.texastribune.org/2025/11/09/texas-greg-abbott-reelection-campaign-governor/ |title=Gov. Greg Abbott launches reelection campaign for fourth term |date=November 9, 2025 |access-date=November 9, 2025 |publisher=[[The Texas Tribune]] |last=Gou |first=Kayla}}</ref> He is being challenged by [[Democratic Party (United States)|Democratic]] state representative [[Gina Hinojosa]].

[[Primary elections]] were held on March 3, 2026. Facing minimal opposition, Abbott won the Republican nomination with 82% of the vote. Hinojosa won the Democratic nomination with 59% of the vote over a field of candidates that included former congressman [[Chris Bell (politician)|Chris Bell]].

If Abbott is re-elected and serves out his fourth term, he will become the state's longest-serving governor. Democrats have not won a gubernatorial election in Texas since [[1990 Texas gubernatorial election|1990]].

A State District Judge ruled in Hinojosa's favor on October 26, granting her access to the documents. Abbott appealed the ruling, accusing Hinojosa "abusing her official capacity as an outgoing lawmaker for political purposes."<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gainey |first=Blaise |date=October 27, 2026 |title=Gina Hinojosa wins court order to review Abbott-Musk emails, Gov.
